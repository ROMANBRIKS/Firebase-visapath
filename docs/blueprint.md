# **App Name**: VisaPath Guides

## Core Features:

- Guide Catalog & Search: Browse and search available visa acquisition guides, filtered by country, visa type, or travel purpose.
- Detailed Guide Pages: View comprehensive information for each guide, including price, table of contents, and prerequisites.
- AI-Powered Guide Matching Tool: An AI-powered tool that recommends the most suitable visa guides based on a user's entered origin, destination, and travel intent.
- Secure Purchase Workflow: Add guides to a cart and complete a secure checkout process.
- User Account & Order History: User registration and login to view past purchases and access downloadable guides.
- Post-Purchase Digital Access: Secure delivery mechanism for users to download their purchased visa guides.
- Admin Guide Management: An administrative interface for adding, editing, and managing visa guide content and details.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual scheme embraces a professional and trustworthy feel. The primary color, a deep and confident blue (#336BDB), evokes reliability and global presence. The background color, a subtle and muted lighter blue (#EAF0F7), provides a calming and focused canvas for guide content. The accent color, a rich and balanced violet (#8B6BDE), adds a touch of sophistication and highlights interactive elements.
- Headlines will utilize 'Space Grotesk', a modern sans-serif, for a clean and contemporary presentation that suggests innovation in travel. Body text will use 'Inter', a neutral and highly readable sans-serif, ensuring clarity and ease of reading for detailed guide information.
- Geometric and crisp line-art icons should be used to represent countries, document types, and travel-related actions, maintaining a professional yet inviting aesthetic.
- A clean, structured layout with ample white space to ensure readability and easy navigation, prioritizing content legibility. Guide listings will use a grid-based approach, and detailed pages will feature a clear content hierarchy.
- Subtle, fluid animations on hover states, form submissions, and content loading will provide positive user feedback without distracting from the informative nature of the guides.
